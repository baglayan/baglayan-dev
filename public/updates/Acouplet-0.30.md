# Acouplet version 0.30 Early Alpha

## Release Notes

- Added charging-case battery reporting to macOS.
- Improved headphone controls, connection feedback, and saved equalizer drafts.
- Improved Find Earbuds warnings when earbud wear status is unknown.
- Improved LDAC volume and mute handling when starting and stopping playback.
- Added an option to remove the LDAC audio driver from Settings.
- Fixed Acouplet reopening after a normal quit.
