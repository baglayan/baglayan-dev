# Acouplet version 0.33 Early Alpha

## Release Notes

- Improved headphone control connections and settings loading.
- Improved LDAC startup, volume control and recovery.
- Added LDAC bitrate and sample-rate changes while enabled.
- Fixed LDAC errors when switching Mac audio outputs.
- Fixed multipoint buttons becoming unavailable during routine refreshes.
- Added playback controls, track information, and separate music and call volume controls on supported devices.
- Expanded noise-control support, including Voice-spotlight and noise-control button settings.
- Added Power Saving, Bluetooth Standby and Automatic Standby settings on supported devices.
- Improved battery reporting in macOS.
- Added missing device images and color variants, and corrected left/right earbud images.
- Expanded equalizer support and fixed layouts that did not fit the screen.
