# Acouplet version 0.31 Early Alpha

## Release Notes

- Improved headphone control connections and recovery.
- Removed LDAC playback’s dependency on Bluetooth system logs.
- Added STAMINA and idle touch-panel lighting settings on supported devices.
- Added recent connection and LDAC events to copied diagnostics. (These are diagnostic information that are temporarily stored locally on your Mac. Acouplet has no telemetry.)
- Fixed switches and links to follow the selected accent color.
- Fixed the menu-bar popover reopening after being closed.
