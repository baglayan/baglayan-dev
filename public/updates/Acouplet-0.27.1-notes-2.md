# Acouplet version 0.27.1 Early Alpha

## Release Notes

- LDAC now selects Sound Quality before starting and restores a previous Stable Connection preference when stopped.
- Clearer errors when the headphones do not offer LDAC, with more detail in connection diagnostics.
- Launch at login is enabled by default; experimental LDAC controls are off by default.
- Fixed the disk image layout and hidden background artwork.
